Course Description

Spark your child's imagination and turn their screen time into a valuable learning experience! In "Creative Coding: Build Your First Games", students embark on an exciting adventure to become game creators.

Through bite-sized, easy-to-follow video lessons, Mr. Alex guides students step-by-step as they design their own playable games. They won't just learn how to code; they'll learn how to think critically, experiment fearlessly, and bring their imaginative ideas to life in a supportive, fun environment.
